Final-year BSc Computer Science and Engineering student at American International University-Bangladesh (AIUB), expected to graduate in January 2027. I work on machine learning research, with a focus on self-supervised representation learning for time series and images.

## Research interests

- Self-supervised and representation learning
- Time-series modelling, forecasting and anomaly detection
- Image classification for medical and agricultural applications
- Entity resolution for Bangla and English text
